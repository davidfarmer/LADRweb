Definition 6.60. pseudoinverse, \(T^\dagger\).

Suppose that \(V\) is finite-dimensional and \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{.}\) The pseudoinverse \(T^\dagger \in \Lc(W{}, V)\) of \(T\) is the linear map from \(W\) to \(V\) defined by
\begin{equation*} T^\dagger w = (T|_{(\ker T)^\perp})^{-1} P_{\!\ran T} w \end{equation*}
for each \(w \in W{}\text{.}\)
