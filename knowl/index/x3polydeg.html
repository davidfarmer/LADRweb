Exercise 28.

Suppose \(D \in \Lc\bigl(\P(\R{})\bigr)\) is such that \(\deg Dp = (\deg p) - 1\) for every nonconstant polynomial \(p \in \P(\R{})\text{.}\) Prove that \(D\) is surjective. Note: The notation \(D\) is used above to remind you of the differentiation map that sends a polynomial \(p\) to \(p'\!\text{.}\)
