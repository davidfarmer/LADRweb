Notation 5.12. \(T^m\).

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(m\) is a positive integer.
  1. \(T^m \in \Lc(V)\) is defined by \(T^m = \underbrace{T \dotsm T}_{m \textit{ times}}{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(T^0\) is defined to be the identity operator \(I\) on \(V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. If \(T\) is invertible with inverse \(T^{-1}{}\text{,}\) then \(T^{-m} \in \Lc(V)\) is defined by
    \begin{equation*} T^{-m} = ( T^{-1} )^m\!. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
