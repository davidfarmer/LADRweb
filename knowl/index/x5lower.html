Exercise 10.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V{}\text{.}\) Show that the following are equivalent.
  1. The matrix of \(T\) with respect to \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is lower triangular.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\Span(v_k, \dots, v_n)\) is invariant under \(T\) for each \(k = 1, \dots, n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(Tv_k \in \Span(v_k, \dots, v_n)\) for each \(k = 1, \dots, n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Note: A square matrix is called lower triangular if all entries above the diagonal are \(0\text{.}\)
