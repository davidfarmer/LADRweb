Theorem 3.84. change-of-basis formula.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Suppose \(u_1, \dots, u_n\) and \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) are bases of \(V{}\text{.}\) Let
\begin{equation*} A= \M\bigl(T, (u_1, \dots, u_n)\bigr) \andd B = \M\bigl(T, (v_1, \dots, v_n)\bigr) \end{equation*}
and \(C = \M\bigl(I, (u_1, \dots, u_n), (v_1, \dots, v_n)\bigr)\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} A = C^{-1}B C. \end{equation*}
