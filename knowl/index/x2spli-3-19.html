Definition 2.11. degree of a polynomial, \(\deg p\).

  1. A polynomial \(p \in \P(\F{})\) is said to have degree \(m\) if there exist scalars \(a_0, a_1, \dots, a_m \in \F{}\) with \(a_m \ne 0\) such that for every \(z \in \F{}\text{,}\) we have
    \begin{equation*} p(z) = a_0 + a_1 z + \dots + a_m z^m\!. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The polynomial that is identically \(0\) is said to have degree \(-\infty\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. The degree of a polynomial \(p\) is denoted by \(\deg p\text{.}\)
