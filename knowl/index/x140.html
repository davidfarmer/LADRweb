Theorem 5.29. \(q(T) = 0 \iff q\) is a polynomial multiple of the minimal polynomial.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional, \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{,}\) and \(q \in \P(\F{})\text{.}\) Then \(q(T) = 0\) if and only if \(q\) is a polynomial multiple of the minimal polynomial of \(T{}\text{.}\)
