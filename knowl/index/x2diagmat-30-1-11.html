Exercise 10.

Give an example of two commuting operators \(S, T\) on a finite-dimensional real vector space such that \(S+T\) has an eigenvalue that does not equal an eigenvalue of \(S\) plus an eigenvalue of \(T\) and \(ST\) has an eigenvalue that does not equal an eigenvalue of \(S\) times an eigenvalue of \(T{}\text{.}\) Note: This exercise shows that Theorem 5.67 does not hold on real vector spaces.
