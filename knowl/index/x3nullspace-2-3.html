Definition 3.11. null space, \(\ker T\).

For \(T \in \Lc(V{},W)\text{,}\) the null space of \(T\text{,}\) denoted by \(\ker T\text{,}\) is the subset of \(V\) consisting of those vectors that \(T\) maps to \(0\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \ker T = \{v \in V: Tv = 0 \}. \end{equation*}
