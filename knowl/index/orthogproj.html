Definition 6.50. orthogonal projection, \(P_{U}\).

Suppose \(U\) is a finite-dimensional subspace of \(V{}\text{.}\) The orthogonal projection of \(V\) onto \(U\) is the operator \(P_{\!U} \in \Lc(V)\) defined as follows: For each \(v \in V{}\text{,}\) write \(v = u + w\text{,}\) where \(u \in U\) and \(w \in U^\perp{}\text{.}\) Then let \(P_{\!U} v = u\text{.}\)
