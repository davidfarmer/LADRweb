Definition 3.97. addition and scalar multiplication on \(V/U\).

Suppose \(U\) is a subspace of \(V{}\text{.}\) Then addition and scalar multiplication are defined on \(V{}/U\) by
\begin{align*} (v + U) + (w + U) \amp = (v+w) + U\\ \lambda(v + U) \amp = (\lambda v) + U \end{align*}
for all \(v, w \in V\) and all \(\lambda \in \F{}{}\text{.}\)
