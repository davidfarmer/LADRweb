Theorem 6.53. Riesz representation theorem, revisited.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional. For each \(v \in V{}\text{,}\) define \(\phi_v \in V'\) by
\begin{equation*} \phi_v(u) = \langle u, v \rangle \end{equation*}
for each \(u \in V{}\text{.}\) Then \(v \mapsto \phi_v\) is a one-to-one function from \(V\) onto \(V'\!\text{.}\)
