Definition 3.79. identity matrix, I.

Suppose \(n\) is a positive integer. The \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix
\begin{equation*} \begin{pmatrix}1 \amp \amp 0 \\ \amp \ddots \amp \\ 0 \amp \amp 1 \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
with \(1\)’s on the diagonal (the entries where the row number equals the column number) and \(0\)’s elsewhere is called the identity matrix and is denoted by \(I\text{.}\)
