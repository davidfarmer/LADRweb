Example 3.20. surjectivity depends on the target space.

The differentiation map \(D \in \Lc\bigl(\P{}_5(\R{})\bigr)\) defined by \(Dp = p'\) is not surjective, because the polynomial \(x^5\) is not in the range of \(D\text{.}\) However, the differentiation map \(S \in \Lc\bigl(\P{}_5(\R{}), \P{}_4(\R{})\bigr)\) defined by \(Sp = p'\) is surjective, because its range equals \(\P{}_4(\R{})\text{,}\) which is the vector space into which \(S\) maps.
