Definition 3.27. matrix, \(A_{j,k}\).

Suppose \(m\) and \(n\) are nonnegative integers. An \(m\)-by-\(n\) matrix \(A\) is a rectangular array of elements of \(\F{}\) with \(m\) rows and \(n\) columns:
\begin{equation*} A = \begin{pmatrix}A_{1,1} \amp \cdots \amp A_{1,n} \\ \vdots \amp \amp \vdots \\ A_{m,1} \amp \cdots \amp A_{m,n} \end{pmatrix} . \end{equation*}
🔗
The notation \(A_{j,k}\) denotes the entry in row \(j\text{,}\) column \(k\) of \(A\text{.}\)
