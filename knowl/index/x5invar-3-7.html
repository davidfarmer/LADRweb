Notation 5.13. \(p(T)\).

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(p \in \P(\F{})\) is a polynomial given by
\begin{equation*} p(z) = a_0 + a_1 z + a_2 z^2 + \dots + a_m z^m \end{equation*}
for all \(z \in \F{}{}\text{.}\) Then \(p(T)\) is the operator on \(V\) defined by
\begin{equation*} p(T) = a_0 I + a_1 T + a_2 T^2 + \dots + a_m T^m\!. \end{equation*}
