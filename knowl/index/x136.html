Theorem 5.27. eigenvalues are the zeros of the minimal polynomial.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\)
  1. The zeros of the minimal polynomial of \(T\) are the eigenvalues of \(T{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(V\) is a complex vector space, then the minimal polynomial of \(T\) has the form
    \begin{equation*} (z-\lambda_1) \cdots (z-\lambda_m), \end{equation*}
    where \(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_m\) is a list of all eigenvalues of \(T\text{,}\) possibly with repetitions.
