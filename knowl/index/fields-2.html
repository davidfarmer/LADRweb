A field is a set containing at least two distinct elements called \(0\) and \(1\text{,}\) along with operations of addition and multiplication satisfying all properties listed in Theorem 1.3. Thus \(\R{}\) and \(\C{}\) are fields, as is the set of rational numbers along with the usual operations of addition and multiplication. Another example of a field is the set \(\{0, 1\}\) with the usual operations of addition and multiplication except that \(1 + 1\) is defined to equal \(0\text{.}\)
