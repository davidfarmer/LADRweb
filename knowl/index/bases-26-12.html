Exercise 11.

Suppose \(V\) is a real vector space. Show that if \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V\) (as a real vector space), then \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is also a basis of the complexification \(V{}_{\C{}}\) (as a complex vector space). Note: See Exercise 1.2.8.b in Section 1.2 for the definition of the complexification \(V{}_{\C{}}\text{.}\)
