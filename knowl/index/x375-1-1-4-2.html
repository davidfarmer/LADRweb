Item 6.2.2.8.2.

The differentiation operator \big(the operator that takes \(p\) to \(p'\)\big) on \(\P{}_2(\R{})\) has an upper-triangular matrix with respect to the basis \(1, x, x^2{}\text{,}\) which is not an orthonormal basis. Find the matrix of the differentiation operator on \(\P{}_2(\R{})\) with respect to the orthonormal basis produced in (a) and verify that this matrix is upper triangular, as expected from the proof of Theorem 6.36.
