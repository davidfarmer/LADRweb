Definition 6.43. orthogonal complement, \(U^\perp\).

If \(U\) is a subset of \(V{}\text{,}\) then the orthogonal complement of \(U\text{,}\) denoted by \(U^\perp{}\text{,}\) is the set of all vectors in \(V\) that are orthogonal to every vector in \(U\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} U^\perp = \{ v \in V: \langle u,v \rangle = 0 \ for every\ u \in U \}. \end{equation*}
