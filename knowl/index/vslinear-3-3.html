Definition 3.5. addition and scalar multiplication on \(\Lc(V,W)\).

Suppose \(S, T \in \Lc(V{},W)\) and \(\lambda \in \F{}{}\text{.}\) The sum \(S + T\) and the product \(\lambda T\) are the linear maps from \(V\) to \(W\) defined by
\begin{equation*} (S+T)(v) = Sv + Tv \andd (\lambda T)(v) = \lambda (Tv) \end{equation*}
for all \(v \in V{}\text{.}\)
