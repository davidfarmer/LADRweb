Exercise 30.

Suppose \(V\) is a real inner product space. For \(u, v, w, x\in V{}\text{,}\) define
\begin{equation*} \langle u+iv, w + ix \rangle _{\C{}} = \langle u,w \rangle + \langle v,x \rangle + (\langle v,w \rangle - \langle u,x \rangle )i. \end{equation*}
  1. Show that \(\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle _{\C{}}\) makes \(V{}_{\C{}}\) into a complex inner product space.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Show that if \(u, v \in V{}\text{,}\) then
    \begin{equation*} \langle u, v \rangle _{\C{}} = \langle u, v \rangle \andd {\lVert u + iv \rVert _{\C{}}}^{2} = \lVert u \rVert ^2 + \lVert v \rVert ^2{}. \end{equation*}
