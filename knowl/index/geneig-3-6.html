Definition 8.8. generalized eigenvector.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(\lambda\) is an eigenvalue of \(T{}\text{.}\) A vector \(v \in V\) is called a generalized eigenvector of \(T\) corresponding to \(\lambda\) if \(v \ne 0\) and
\begin{equation*} (T - \lambda I)^k v = 0 \end{equation*}
for some positive integer \(k\text{.}\)
