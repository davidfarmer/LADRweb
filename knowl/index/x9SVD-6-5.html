Definition 7.108. volume of a box.

Suppose \(\F{} = \R{}\text{.}\) If \(u \in V\) and \(r_1, \ldots, r_n\) are positive numbers and \(e_1, \ldots, e_n\) is an orthonormal basis of \(V{}\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \vol ( u + P(r_1 e_1, \ldots, r_n e_n) ) = r_1 \times \cdots \times r_n. \end{equation*}
