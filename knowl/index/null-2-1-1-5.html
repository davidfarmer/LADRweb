Item e.

Suppose \(T\in \Lc( \F{\infty} )\) is the backward shift defined by
\begin{equation*} T(x_1, x_2, x_3, \dots) = (x_2, x_3, \dots). \end{equation*}
Then \(T(x_1, x_2, x_3, \dots)\) equals \(0\) if and only if the numbers \(x_2, x_3, \dots\) are all \(0\text{.}\) Thus \(\ker T = \{ (a, 0, 0, \dots): a \in \F{} \}\text{.}\)
