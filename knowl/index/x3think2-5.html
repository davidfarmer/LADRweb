Exercise 4.

Suppose that \(D \in \Lc\bigl(\P{}_3(\R{}), \P{}_2(\R{}) \bigr)\) is the differentiation map defined by \(Dp = p'\!\text{.}\) Find a basis of \(\P{}_3(\R{})\) and a basis of \(\P{}_2(\R{})\) such that the matrix of \(D\) with respect to these bases is
\begin{equation*} \begin{pmatrix}1 \amp 0 \amp 0 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 0 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp 0 \amp 1 \amp 0 \\ \end{pmatrix} . \end{equation*}
