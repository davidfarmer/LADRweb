Definition 7.97. ellipsoid, \(E(s_1 f_1, \ldots, s_n f_n)\text{,}\) principal axes.

Suppose that \(f_1, \ldots, f_n\) is an orthonormal basis of \(V\) and \(s_1, \ldots, s_n\) are positive numbers. The ellipsoid \(E(s_1 f_1, \ldots, s_n f_n)\) with principal axes \(s_1 f_1, \ldots, s_n f_n\) is defined by
\begin{equation*} E(s_1 f_1, \ldots, s_n f_n) = \{ v \in V:\frac{\lvert \langle v,f_1 \rangle \rvert ^2}{{s_1}^{2}} + \cdots + \frac{\lvert \langle v,f_n \rangle \rvert ^2}{{s_n}^{2}} \lt 1 \}. \end{equation*}
