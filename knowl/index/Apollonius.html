Exercise 34.

Use inner products to prove Apollonius’s identity: In a triangle with sides of length \(a\text{,}\) \(b\text{,}\) and \(c\text{,}\) let \(d\) be the length of the line segment from the midpoint of the side of length \(c\) to the opposite vertex. Then
\begin{equation*} a^2 + b^2 = \tfrac{1}{2} c^2 + 2d^2{}. \end{equation*}
