Exercise 8.

Suppose \(U\) is a finite-dimensional subspace of \(V\) and \(v \in V{}\text{.}\) Define a linear functional \(\fun \phi U \F{}\) by
\begin{equation*} \phi(u) = \langle u, v \rangle \end{equation*}
for all \(u \in U\text{.}\) By the Riesz representation theorem Theorem 6.41 as applied to the inner product space \(U\text{,}\) there exists a unique vector \(w \in U\) such that
\begin{equation*} \phi(u) = \langle u, w \rangle \end{equation*}
for all \(u \in U\text{.}\) Show that \(w = P_{\!U} v\text{.}\)
