Exercise 8.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_m\) are the distinct eigenvalues of \(T{}\text{.}\) Prove that
\begin{equation*} V = G(\lambda_1, T) \oplus \cdots \oplus G(\lambda_m, T) \end{equation*}
if and only if the minimal polynomial of \(T\) equals \((z-\lambda_1)^{k_1} \cdots (z - \lambda_m)^{k_m}\) for some positive integers \(k_1, \ldots, k_m\text{.}\) Note: The case \(\F{} = \C{}\) follows immediately from Theorem 5.24 (b) and the generalized eigenspace decomposition Theorem 8.19 ; thus this exercise is interesting only when \(\F{} = \R{}\text{.}\)
