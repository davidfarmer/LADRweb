Exercise 19.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(\lVert T^*v \rVert \le \| Tv \|\) for every \(v \in V{}\text{.}\) Prove that \(T\) is normal. Note: This exercise fails on infinite-dimensional inner product spaces, leading to what are called hyponormal operators, which have a well-developed theory.
