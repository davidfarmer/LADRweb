Definition 6.2. inner product.

An inner product on \(V\) is a function that takes each ordered pair \((u,v)\) of elements of \(V\) to a number \(\langle u,v \rangle \in \F{}\) and has the following properties.
  1. positivity.
    \(\langle v, v \rangle \ge 0\) for all \(v \in V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. definiteness.
    \(\langle v, v \rangle = 0\) if and only if \(v = 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. additivity in first slot.
    \(\langle u + v, w \rangle = \langle u, w \rangle + \langle v, w \rangle\) for all \(u, v, w \in V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. homogeneity in first slot.
    \(\langle \lambda u, v \rangle = \lambda \langle u, v \rangle\) for all \(\lambda \in \F{}\) and all \(u, v \in V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. conjugate symmetry.
    \(\langle u, v \rangle = \overline{\langle v,u \rangle }\) for all \(u, v \in V{}\text{.}\)
