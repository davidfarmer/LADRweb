Theorem 7.26. complex spectral theorem.

Suppose \(\F{} = \C{}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Then the following are equivalent.
  1. \(T\) is normal.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(T\) has a diagonal matrix with respect to some orthonormal basis of \(V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(V\) has an orthonormal basis consisting of eigenvectors of \(T{}\text{.}\)
