Exercise 20.

Suppose \(\F{}=\C{}\text{,}\) \(V\) is finite-dimensional, and \(\E \subset \Lc(V)\) is such that
\begin{equation*} ST = TS \end{equation*}
for all \(S, T \in \E\text{.}\) Prove that there is an orthonormal basis of \(V\) with respect to which every element of \(\E\) has an upper-triangular matrix. Note: This exercise strengthens Exercise 5.5.9 (b) in Section 5.5 (in the context of inner product spaces) by asserting that the basis in that exercise can be chosen to be orthonormal.
