Exercise 22.

Suppose \(C[-1, 1]\) is the vector space of continuous real-valued functions on the interval \([-1 ,1]\) with inner product given by
\begin{equation*} \langle f, g \rangle = \int_{-1}^1 fg \end{equation*}
for all \(f, g \in C[-1, 1]\text{.}\) Let \(\phi\) be the linear functional on \(C[-1, 1]\) defined by \(\phi(f) = f(0)\text{.}\) Show that there does not exist \(g \in C[-1, 1]\) such that
\begin{equation*} \phi(f) = \langle f, g \rangle \end{equation*}
for every \(f \in C[-1, 1]\text{.}\) Note: This exercise shows that the Riesz representation theorem Theorem 6.41 does not hold on infinite-dimensional vector spaces without additional hypotheses on \(V\) and \(\phi\text{.}\)
