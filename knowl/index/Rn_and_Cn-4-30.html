Definition 1.17. additive inverse in \(\F{n}\text{,}\) \(-x\).

For \(x \in \FF{n}\text{,}\) the additive inverse of \(x\text{,}\) denoted by \(-x\text{,}\) is the vector \(-x \in \F{n}\) such that
\begin{equation*} x + (-x) = 0. \end{equation*}
🔗
Thus if \(x= (x_1, \dots, x_n)\text{,}\) then \(-x = (-x_1, \dots, -x_n)\text{.}\)
