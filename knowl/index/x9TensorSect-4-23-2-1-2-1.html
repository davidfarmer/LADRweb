Suppose that \(\fun \Gamma {V{}_1 \times \cdots \times V{}_m} U\) is an \(m\)-linear map. Then there exists a unique linear map \(\fun {\hat{\Gamma}} {V{}_1 \tensor \cdots \tensor V{}_m} U\) such that
\begin{equation*} \hat{\Gamma}(v_1 \tensor \cdots \tensor v_m) = \Gamma(v_1, \ldots, v_m) \end{equation*}
for all \((v_1, \ldots, v_m) \in V{}_1 \times \cdots \times V{}_m\text{.}\)
