Definition 3.73. matrix of a vector, \(\M(v)\).

Suppose \(v \in V\) and \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V{}\text{.}\) The matrix of \(v\) with respect to this basis is the \(n\)-by-\(1\) matrix
\begin{equation*} \M(v) = \begin{pmatrix}b_1 \\ \vdots \\ b_n \end{pmatrix} , \end{equation*}
where \(b_1, \dots, b_n\) are the scalars such that
\begin{equation*} v = b_1 v_1 + \dots + b_n v_n. \end{equation*}
