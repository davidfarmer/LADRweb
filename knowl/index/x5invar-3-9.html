Example 5.15. a polynomial applied to the differentiation operator.

Suppose \(D \in \Lc\bigl(\P(\R{})\bigr)\) is the differentiation operator defined by \(Dq = q'\) and \(p\) is the polynomial defined by \(p(x) = 7 - 3x + 5x^2{}\text{.}\) Then \(p(D) = 7I - 3D + 5D^2{}\text{.}\) Thus
\begin{equation*} \bigl(p(D)\bigr)q = 7q - 3q' + 5q'' \end{equation*}
for every \(q \in \P(\R{})\text{.}\)
