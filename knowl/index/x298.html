Theorem 3.81. matrix of product of linear maps.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(U, V)\) and \(S \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{.}\) If \(u_1, \dots, u_m\) is a basis of \(U\text{,}\) \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V{}\text{,}\) and \(w_1, \dots, w_p\) is a basis of \(W{}\text{,}\) then
\begin{align*} \M\bigl(ST\amp , (u_1, \dots, u_m), (w_1, \dots, w_p) \bigr) =\\ \amp \M\bigl(S, (v_1, \dots, v_n), (w_1, \dots, w_p) \bigr) \M\bigl(T, (u_1, \dots, u_m), (v_1, \dots, v_n) \bigr)\text{.} \end{align*}
