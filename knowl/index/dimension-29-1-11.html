Exercise 10.

Suppose \(m\) is a positive integer. For \(0 \le k \le m\text{,}\) let
\begin{equation*} p_k(x) = x^k (1-x)^{m-k}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Show that \(p_0, \ldots, p_m\) is a basis of \(\P{}_m(\F{})\text{.}\) Note: The basis in this exercise leads to what are called Bernstein polynomials. You can do a web search to learn how Bernstein polynomials are used to approximate continuous functions on \([0,1]\text{.}\)
