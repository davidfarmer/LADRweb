Definition 6.38. linear functional, dual space, \(V'\).

  1. A linear functional on \(V\) is a linear map from \(V\) to \(\F{}{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The dual space of \(V{}\text{,}\) denoted by \(V'\!\text{,}\) is the vector space of all linear functionals on \(V{}\text{.}\) In other words, \(V' = \Lc(V{}, \F{})\text{.}\)
