Definition 5.31. matrix of an operator, \(\M(T)\).

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) The matrix of \(T\) with respect to a basis \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) of \(V\) is the \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix
\begin{equation*} \M(T) = \begin{pmatrix}A_{1,1} \amp \cdots \amp A_{1,n} \\ \vdots \amp \amp \vdots \\ A_{n,1} \amp \cdots \amp A_{n,n} \end{pmatrix} \end{equation*}
whose entries \(A_{j,k}\) are defined by
\begin{equation*} Tv_k = A_{1,k} v_1 + \dots + A_{n,k} v_n. \end{equation*}
🔗
The notation \(\M\bigl( T, (v_1, \dots, v_n) \bigr)\) is used if the basis is not clear from the context.
