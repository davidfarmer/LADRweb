Exercise 14.

Suppose \(\F{} = \C{}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Prove that there does not exist a direct sum decomposition of \(V\) into two nonzero subspaces invariant under \(T\) if and only if the minimal polynomial of \(T\) is of the form \((z - \lambda)^{\dim V}\) for some \(\lambda \in \C{}\text{.}\)
