Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) If \(m \ge 2\) and
\begin{equation*} V = V{}_1 \oplus \dots \oplus V{}_m , \end{equation*}
where each \(V{}_k\) is a nonzero subspace of \(V{}\text{,}\) then to understand the behavior of \(T\) we only need to understand the behavior of each \(T|_{V{}_k}\text{;}\) here \(T|_{V{}_k}\) denotes the restriction of \(T\) to the smaller domain \(V{}_k\text{.}\) Dealing with \(T|_{V{}_k}\) should be easier than dealing with \(T\) because \(V{}_k\) is a smaller vector space than \(V{}\text{.}\)
