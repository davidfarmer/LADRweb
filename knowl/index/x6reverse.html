Exercise 20.

Prove that if \(u, v \in V{}\text{,}\) then \(\lvert \lVert u \rVert - \lVert v \rVert \rvert \le \lVert u-v \rVert\text{.}\) Note: That inequality is called the reverse triangle inequality. For the reverse triangle inequality when \(V = \C{}\text{,}\) see Exercise 4.2 in Chapter 4.
