Definition 1.41. sum of subspaces.

Suppose \(V{}_1, \dots, V{}_m\) are subspaces of \(V{}\text{.}\) The sum of \(V{}_1, \dots, V{}_m\text{,}\) denoted by \(V{}_1 + \dots + V{}_m\text{,}\) is the set of all possible sums of elements of \(V{}_1, \dots, V{}_m\text{.}\) More precisely,
\begin{equation*} V{}_1 + \dots + V{}_m = \{ v_1 + \dots + v_m: v_1 \in V{}_1, \dots, v_m \in V{}_m \}. \end{equation*}
