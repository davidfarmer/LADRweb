Theorem 5.39. conditions for upper-triangular matrix.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V{}\text{.}\) Then the following are equivalent.
  1. The matrix of \(T\) with respect to \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is upper triangular.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\Span(v_1, \dots, v_k)\) is invariant under \(T\) for each \(k = 1, \dots, n\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(Tv_k \in \Span(v_1, \dots, v_k)\) for each \(k = 1, \dots, n\text{.}\)

    🔗
