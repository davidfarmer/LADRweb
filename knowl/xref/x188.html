Theorem 6.37. upper-triangular matrix with respect to some orthonormal basis.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Then \(T\) has an upper-triangular matrix with respect to some orthonormal basis of \(V\) if and only if the minimal polynomial of \(T\) equals \((z-\lambda_1) \cdots (z-\lambda_m)\) for some \(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_m \in \F{}{}\text{.}\)
