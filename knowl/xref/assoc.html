Exercise 12.

Prove that matrix multiplication is associative. In other words, suppose \(A\text{,}\) \(B\text{,}\) and \(C\) are matrices whose sizes are such that \((AB)C\) makes sense. Explain why \(A(BC)\) makes sense and prove that
\begin{equation*} (AB)C = A(BC). \end{equation*}
🔗
Note: Try to find a clean proof that illustrates the following quote from Emil Artin: “It is my experience that proofs involving matrices can be shortened by 50% if one throws the matrices out.”
