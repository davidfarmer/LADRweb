Exercise 2.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{,}\) \(m\) is a positive integer, \(v \in V\text{,}\) and \(T^{m-1} v \ne 0\) but \(T^m v = 0\text{.}\) Prove that \(v, Tv, T^2 v, \dots, T^{m-1} v\) is linearly independent. Note: The result in this exercise is used in the proof of Theorem 8.46.
