Exercise 25.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional, \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{,}\) and \(U\) is a subspace of \(V\) that is invariant under \(T{}\text{.}\)
  1. Prove that the minimal polynomial of \(T\) is a polynomial multiple of the minimal polynomial of the quotient operator \(T{}/U\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Prove that
    \begin{equation*} \text{(minimal polynomial of \(T|_U\)) \(\times\) (minimal polynomial of \(T{}/U\))} \end{equation*}
    is a polynomial multiple of the minimal polynomial of \(T{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
