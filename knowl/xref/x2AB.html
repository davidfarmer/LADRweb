Exercise 2.

Suppose \(A\) and \(B\) are upper-triangular matrices of the same size, with \(\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\) on the diagonal of \(A\) and \(\beta_1, \ldots, \beta_n\) on the diagonal of \(B\text{.}\)
  1. Show that \(A+B\) is an upper-triangular matrix with \(\alpha_1 + \beta _1, \ldots, \alpha_n + \beta_n\) on the diagonal.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Show that \(AB\) is an upper-triangular matrix with \(\alpha_1 \beta _1, \ldots, \alpha_n \beta_n\) on the diagonal.

    🔗
🔗
