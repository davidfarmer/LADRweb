Exercise 15.

Prove that if \(A\) is an \(m\)-by-\(n\) matrix and \(C\) is an \(n\)-by-\(p\) matrix, then
\begin{equation*} \tran{(AC)} = \tran{C} \tran{A}{}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Note: This exercise shows that the transpose of the product of two matrices is the product of the transposes in the opposite order.
