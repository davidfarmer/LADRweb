Theorem 3.86. products and direct sums.

Suppose that \(V{}_1, \dots, V{}_m\) are subspaces of \(V{}\text{.}\) Define a linear map \(\Gamma: V{}_1 \times \dots \times V{}_m \to V{}_1 + \dots + V{}_m\) by
\begin{equation*} \Gamma(v_1, \dots, v_m) = v_1 + \dots + v_m. \end{equation*}
🔗
Then \(V{}_1 + \dots + V{}_m\) is a direct sum if and only if \(\Gamma\) is injective.
