Theorem 9.62. if \(\F{} = \C{}\text{,}\) then characteristic polynomial of \(T\) equals \(\det(z I - T)\).

Suppose \(\F{} = \C{}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Let \(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_m\) denote the distinct eigenvalues of \(T\text{,}\) and let \(d_1, \ldots, d_m\) denote their multiplicities. Then
\begin{equation*} \det(zI - T) = (z - \lambda_1)^{d_1} \dotsm (z - \lambda_m)^{d_m}\!. \end{equation*}
