Exercise 21.

Suppose \(\dim V > 1\) and \(\dim W > 1\text{.}\) Prove that the norm on \(\Lc(V{}, W)\) does not come from an inner product. In other words, prove that there does not exist an inner product on \(\Lc(V{}, W)\) such that
\begin{equation*} \max \{ \lVert Tv \rVert : v \in V \text{ and } \lVert v \rVert \le 1 \} = {\textstyle \sqrt{\langle T, T \rangle }} \end{equation*}
for all \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{.}\)
