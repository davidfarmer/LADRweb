Exercise 19.

Suppose \(v_1, \ldots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Prove that if \(\lambda\) is an eigenvalue of \(T\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \lvert \lambda \rvert ^2 \le \sum_{j=1}^n \sum_{k=1}^n \lvert \M(T)_{j,k} \rvert ^2{}, \end{equation*}
where \(\M(T)_{j,k}\) denotes the entry in row \(j\text{,}\) column \(k\) of the matrix of \(T\) with respect to the basis \(v_1, \ldots, v_n\text{.}\)
