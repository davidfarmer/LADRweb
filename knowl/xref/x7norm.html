Definition 7.86. norm of a linear map, \(\lVert \cdot \rVert\).

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{.}\) Then the norm of \(T\text{,}\) denoted by \(\lVert T \rVert\text{,}\) is defined by
\begin{equation*} \lVert T \rVert = \max \{ \lVert Tv \rVert : v \in V \text{ and } \lVert v \rVert \le 1 \}. \end{equation*}
