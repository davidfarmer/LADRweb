Example 3.114. the dual basis of the standard basis of \(\F{n}\).

Suppose \(n\) is a positive integer. For \(1 \le j \le n\text{,}\) define \(\phi_j\) to be the linear functional on \(\F{n}\) that selects the \(j^\nth\) coordinate of a vector in \(\FF{n}\text{.}\) Thus
\begin{equation*} \phi_j(x_1, \dots, x_n) = x_j \end{equation*}
for each \((x_1, \dots, x_n) \in \FF{n}\text{.}\)
🔗
Let \(e_1, \dots, e_n\) be the standard basis of \(\FF{n}\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} \phi_j(e_k) = \begin{cases}1 \amp \text{ if } k = j,\\ 0 \amp \text{ if } k \ne j. \end{cases} \end{equation*}
🔗
Thus \(\phi_1, \dots, \phi_n\) is the dual basis of the standard basis \(e_1, \dots, e_n\) of \(\FF{n}\text{.}\)
