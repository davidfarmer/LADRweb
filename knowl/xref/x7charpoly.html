Definition 8.23. characteristic polynomial.

Suppose \(\F{} = \C{}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Let \(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m\) denote the distinct eigenvalues of \(T\text{,}\) with multiplicities \(d_1, \dots, d_m\text{.}\) The polynomial
\begin{equation*} (z - \lambda_1)^{d_1} \dotsm (z - \lambda_m)^{d_m} \end{equation*}
is called the characteristic polynomial of \(T{}\text{.}\)
