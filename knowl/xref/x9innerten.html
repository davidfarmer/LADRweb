Theorem 9.77. inner product on tensor product of two inner product spaces.

Suppose \(V\) and \(W\) are inner product spaces. Then there is a unique inner product on \(V{} \tensor W\) such that
\begin{equation*} \langle v \tensor w, u \tensor x \rangle = \langle v, u \rangle \langle w, x \rangle \end{equation*}
for all \(v, u \in V\) and \(w, x \in W{}\text{.}\)
