Exercise 13.

Suppose that \(V{}_1, \ldots, V{}_m\) are finite-dimensional inner product spaces and \(V{}_1 \tensor \cdots \tensor V{}_m\) is made into an inner product space using the inner product from Exercise 9.4.3.12. Suppose \(e_1^{ k}, \ldots, e_{n_k}^{ k}\) is an orthonormal basis of \(V{}_k\) for each \(k = 1, \ldots, m\text{.}\) Show that the list
\begin{equation*} \{ e_{j_1}^1 \tensor \cdots \tensor e_{j_m}^m \}_{j_1 = 1, \ldots, n_1; \cdots ; j_m = 1, \ldots, n_m} \end{equation*}
is an orthonormal basis of \(V{}_1 \tensor \cdots \tensor V{}_m\text{.}\)
