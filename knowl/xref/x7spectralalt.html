Exercise 20.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) is normal and \(U\) is a subspace of  \(V\) that is invariant under  \(T{}\text{.}\)
  1. Prove that \(U^\perp\) is invariant under \(T{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Prove that \(U\) is invariant under \(T^*{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Prove that \((T|_U)^* = ( T^* )|_U\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Prove that \(T|_U \in \Lc(U)\) and \(T|_{U^\perp} \in \Lc( U^\perp )\) are normal operators.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Note: This exercise can be used to give yet another proof of the complex spectral theorem (use induction on \(\dim V\) and the result that \(T\) has an eigenvector) .
