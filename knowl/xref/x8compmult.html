Exercise 23.

Suppose \(\F{} = \R{}\text{,}\) \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{,}\) and \(\lambda \in \C{}\text{.}\)
  1. Show that \(u + iv \in G( \lambda, T{}_{\C{}} )\) if and only if \(u - iv \in G( \overline{\lambda}, T{}_{\C{}} )\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Show that the multiplicity of \(\lambda\) as an eigenvalue of \(T{}_{\C{}}\) equals the multiplicity of \(\bar{\lambda}\) as an eigenvalue of \(T{}_{\C{}}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Use (b) and the result about the sum of the multiplicities Theorem 8.25 to show that if \(\dim V\) is an odd number, then \(T{}_{\C{}}\) has a real eigenvalue.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Use (c) and the result about real eigenvalues of \(T{}_{\C{}}\) (Exercise 5.1.2.17 in Section 5.1) to show that if \(\dim V\) is an odd number, then \(T\) has an eigenvalue (thus giving an alternative proof of Theorem 5.34).
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Note: See Exercise 3.2.3.33 in Section 3.2 for the definition of the complexification \(T{}_{\C{}}\text{.}\)
