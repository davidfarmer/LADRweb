Exercise 8.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{.}\) Suppose \(s_1 \ge s_2 \ge \cdots \ge s_m >0\) and \(e_1, \dots, e_m\) is an orthonormal list in \(V\) and \(f_1, \dots, f_m\) is an orthonormal list in \(W\) such that
\begin{equation*} Tv = s_1 \langle v, e_1 \rangle f_1 + \dots + s_m \langle v, e_m \rangle f_m \end{equation*}
for every \(v \in V{}\text{.}\)
  1. Prove that \(f_1, \dots, f_m\) is an orthonormal basis of \(\ran T{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Prove that \(e_1, \dots, e_m\) is an orthonormal basis of \((\ker T)^\perp\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Prove that \(s_1, \ldots, s_m\) are the positive singular values of \(T{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Prove that if \(k \in \{ 1, \ldots, m \}\text{,}\) then \(e_k\) is an eigenvector of \(T^*T\) with corresponding eigenvalue \({s_k}^{2}{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. Prove that
    \begin{equation*} TT^*w = {s_1}^{2} \langle w, f_1 \rangle f_1 + \cdots + {s_m}^{2} \langle w, f_m \rangle f_m \end{equation*}
    for all \(w \in W{}\text{.}\)
