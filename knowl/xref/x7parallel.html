Definition 7.106. \(P(v_1, \ldots, v_n)\text{,}\) parallelepiped.

Suppose \(v_1, \ldots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V{}\text{.}\) Let
\begin{equation*} P(v_1, \ldots, v_n) = \{ a_1 v_1 + \cdots + a_n v_n: a_1, \ldots, a_n \in (0,1) \}. \end{equation*}
🔗
A parallelepiped is a set of the form \(u + P(v_1, \ldots, v_n)\) for some \(u \in V{}\text{.}\) The vectors \(v_1, \ldots, v_n\) are called the edges of this parallelepiped.
