Theorem 5.47. if \(\symbf{F} = \symbf{C}\text{,}\) then every operator on \(V\) has an upper-triangular matrix.

Suppose \(V\) is a finite-dimensional complex vector space and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Then \(T\) has an upper-triangular matrix with respect to some basis of \(V{}\text{.}\)
