Theorem 4.16. factorization of a polynomial over \(\R{}\).

Suppose \(p \in \P(\R{})\) is a nonconstant polynomial. Then \(p\) has a unique factorization (except for the order of the factors) of the form
\begin{equation*} p(x) = c(x - \lambda_1) \cdots(x - \lambda_m) ( x^2 + b_1 x + c_1 ) \cdots ( x^2 + b_M x + c_M ), \end{equation*}
where \(c, \lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m, b_1, \dots, b_M, c_1, \dots, c_M\in \R{}\text{,}\) with \({b_k}^{2} \lt 4c_k\) for each \(k\text{.}\)
