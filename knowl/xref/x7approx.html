Exercise 22.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{.}\) Let \(n = \dim V\) and let \(s_1 \ge \cdots \ge s_n\) denote the singular values of \(T{}\text{.}\) Prove that if \(1 \le k \le n\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \min \{ \lVert T|_U \rVert : U \text { is a subspace of } V \text{ with } \dim U = k \} = s_{n-k+1}. \end{equation*}
