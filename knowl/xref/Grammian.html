Exercise 20.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(e_1, \ldots, e_n\) is an orthonormal basis of \(V{}\text{.}\) Prove that \(T\) is a positive operator if and only if there exist \(v_1, \ldots, v_n \in V\) such that
\begin{equation*} \langle Te_k, e_j \rangle = \langle v_k, v_j \rangle \end{equation*}
for all \(j, k = 1, \ldots, n\text{.}\) Note: The numbers \(\{ \langle Te_k, e_j \rangle \} _{j, k = 1, \ldots, n}\) are the entries in the matrix of \(T\) with respect to the orthonormal basis \(e_1, \ldots, e_n\text{.}\)
