Theorem 9.36. formula for \((\dim V)\)-linear alternating forms on \(V\).

Let \(n = \dim V{}\text{.}\) Suppose \(e_1, \ldots, e_n\) is a basis of \(V\) and \(v_1, \ldots, v_n \in V{}\text{.}\) For each \(k \in \{ 1, \ldots, n \}\text{,}\) let \(b_{1, k}, \ldots, b_{n, k} \in \F{}\) be such that
\begin{equation*} v_k = \sum_{j=1}^n b_{j,k} e_j. \end{equation*}
🔗
Then
\begin{equation*} \alpha (v_1, \ldots, v_n) = \alpha ( e_1, \ldots, e_n ) \sum_{(j_1, \ldots, j_n) \in \perm n} ( \sign(j_1, \ldots, j_n) ) b_{j_1,1} \cdots b_{j_n,n} \end{equation*}
for every alternating \(n\)-linear form \(\alpha\) on \(V{}\text{.}\)
