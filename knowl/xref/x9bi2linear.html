Theorem 9.76. converting bilinear maps to linear maps.

Suppose \(U\) is a vector space.
  1. Suppose \(\fun \Gamma {V{} \times W} U\) is a bilinear map. Then there exists a unique linear map \(\fun {\hat[8]{\Gamma}} {V{} \tensor W} U\) such that
    \begin{equation*} \hat[8]{\Gamma}(v \tensor w) = \Gamma(v, w) \end{equation*}
    for all \((v, w) \in V{} \times W{}\text{.}\)

    \begin{equation*} \pound{T}(v, w) = T(v \tensor w) \end{equation*}
    for all \((v, w) \in V{} \times W{}\text{.}\)
