Theorem 6.6. basic properties of an inner product.

  1. For each fixed \(v \in V{}\text{,}\) the function that takes \(u \in V\) to \(\langle u,v \rangle\) is a linear map from \(V\) to \(\F{}{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\langle 0, v \rangle = 0\) for every \(v \in V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\langle v, 0 \rangle = 0\) for every \(v \in V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\langle u, v + w \rangle = \langle u , v \rangle + \langle u, w \rangle\) for all \(u, v, w \in V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(\langle u, \lambda v \rangle = \bar{\lambda} \langle u,v \rangle\) for all \(\lambda \in \F{}\) and all \(u, v \in V{}\text{.}\)

    🔗
