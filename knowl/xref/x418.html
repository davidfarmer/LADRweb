Theorem 6.57. properties of orthogonal projection \(P_{U}\).

Suppose \(U\) is a finite-dimensional subspace of \(V{}\text{.}\) Then
  1. \(P_{U} \in \Lc(V)\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(P_{U} u = u\) for every \(u \in U\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(P_{U} w = 0\) for every \(w \in U^\perp\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\ran P_{U} = U\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(\ker P_{U} = U^\perp\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(v - P_{U} v \in U^\perp\) for every \(v \in V\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  7. \({P_{U}}^{2} = P_{U}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  8. \(\|P_{U} v\| \le \|v\|\) for every \(v \in V\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  9. if \(e_1, \dots, e_m\) is an orthonormal basis of \(U\) and \(v \in V{}\text{,}\) then
    \begin{equation*} P_{U} v = \langle v, e_1 \rangle e_1 + \dots + \langle v, e_m \rangle e_m. \end{equation*}
