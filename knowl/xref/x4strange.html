Theorem 6.27. norm of an orthonormal linear combination.

Suppose \(e_1, \dots, e_m\) is an orthonormal list of vectors in \(V{}\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} \| a_1 e_1 + \dots + a_m e_m \|^2 = | a_1|^2 + \dots + |a_m|^2 \end{equation*}
for all \(a_1, \dots, a_m \in \F{}{}\text{.}\)
