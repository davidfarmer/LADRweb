Exercise 15.

Suppose \(\F{} = \C{}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Prove that \(T\) is diagonalizable if and only if every generalized eigenvector of \(T\) is an eigenvector of \(T{}\text{.}\) Note: For \(\F{} = \C{}\text{,}\) this exercise adds another equivalence to the list of conditions for diagonalizability in Theorem 5.49.
