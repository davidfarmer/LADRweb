Definition 3.80. product of vector spaces.

Suppose \(V{}_1, \dots, V{}_m\) are vector spaces over \(\F{}{}\text{.}\)
  1. The product \(V{}_1 \times \dots \times V{}_m\) is defined by
    \begin{equation*} V{}_1 \times \dots \times V{}_m = \{ (v_1, \dots, v_m): v_1 \in V{}_1, \dots, v_m \in V{}_m \}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Addition on \(V{}_1 \times \dots \times V{}_m\) is defined by
    \begin{equation*} (u_1, \dots, u_m) + (v_1, \dots, v_m) = (u_1 + v_1, \dots, u_m + v_m). \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Scalar multiplication on \(V{}_1 \times \dots \times V{}_m\) is defined by
    \begin{equation*} \lambda (v_1, \dots, v_m) = (\lambda v_1, \dots, \lambda v_m). \end{equation*}
