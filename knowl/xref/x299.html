Theorem 3.82. matrix of identity operator with respect to two bases.

Suppose that \(u_1, \dots, u_n\) and \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) are bases of \(V{}\text{.}\) Then the matrices
\begin{equation*} \M\bigl(I, (u_1, \dots, u_n), (v_1, \dots, v_n)\bigr) \andd \M\bigl(I, (v_1, \dots, v_n), (u_1, \dots, u_n)\bigr) \end{equation*}
are invertible, and each is the inverse of the other.
