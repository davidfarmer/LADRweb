Exercise 21.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional, \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{,}\) and \(U\) is a subspace of \(W{}\text{.}\) Prove that \(\{ v \in V: Tv \in U \}\) is a subspace of \(V\) and
\begin{equation*} \dim \{ v \in V: Tv \in U \} = \dim \ker T + \dim (U \cap \ran T). \end{equation*}
