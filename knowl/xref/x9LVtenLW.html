Exercise 9.

Suppose \(S \in \Lc(V)\) and \(T \in \Lc(W)\text{.}\) Prove that there exists a unique operator on \(V{} \tensor W\) that takes \(v \tensor w\) to \(Sv \tensor Tw\) for all \(v \in V\) and \(w \in W{}\text{.}\) Note: In an abuse of notation, the operator on \(V{} \tensor W\) given by this exercise is often called \(S \tensor T{}\text{.}\)
