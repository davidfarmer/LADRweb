Exercise 11.

Prove that the distributive property holds for matrix addition and matrix multiplication. In other words, suppose \(A\text{,}\) \(B\text{,}\) \(C\text{,}\) \(D\text{,}\) \(E\text{,}\) and \(F\) are matrices whose sizes are such that \(A(B+C)\) and \((D+E)F\) make sense. Explain why \(AB + AC\) and \(DF + EF\) both make sense and prove that
\begin{equation*} A(B+C) = AB + AC \andd (D+E)F = DF + EF. \end{equation*}
