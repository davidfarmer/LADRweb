Exercise 4.

Suppose \(V\) is a real inner product space and \(\beta\) is a bilinear form on \(V{}\text{.}\) Show that there exists a unique operator \(T \in \Lc(V)\) such that
\begin{equation*} \beta(u, v) = \langle u,Tv \rangle \end{equation*}
for all \(u, v \in V{}\text{.}\) Note: This exercise states that if \(V\) is a real inner product space, then every bilinear form on \(V\) is of the form given by the third bullet point in Example 9.2.
