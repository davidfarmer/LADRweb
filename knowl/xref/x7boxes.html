Theorem 7.90. every invertible operator takes some boxes to boxes.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) is invertible. Suppose \(T\) has singular value decomposition
\begin{equation*} Tv = s_1 \langle v, e_1 \rangle f_1 + \cdots + s_n \langle v, e_n \rangle f_n, \end{equation*}
where \(s_1, \ldots, s_n\) are the singular values of \(T\) and \(e_1, \ldots, e_n\) and \(f_1, \ldots, f_n\) are orthonormal bases of \(V\) and the equation above holds for all \(v \in V{}\text{.}\) Then \(T\) maps the box \(u + P(r_1 e_1, \ldots, r_ne_n)\) onto the box \(Tu + P(r_1 s_1 f_1, \ldots, r_n s_n f_n)\) for all positive numbers \(r_1, \ldots, r_n\) and all \(u \in V{}\text{.}\)
