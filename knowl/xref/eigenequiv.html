Theorem 5.7. equivalent conditions to be an eigenvalue.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional, \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{,}\) and \(\lambda \in \F{}{}\text{.}\) Then the following are equivalent.
  1. \(\lambda\) is an eigenvalue of \(T{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(T - \lambda I\) is not injective.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(T - \lambda I\) is not surjective.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(T - \lambda I\) is not invertible.
