Theorem 6.33. writing a vector as a linear combination of an orthonormal basis.

Suppose \(e_1, \dots, e_n\) is an orthonormal basis of \(V\) and \(u, v \in V{}\text{.}\) Then
  1. \(v = \langle v, e_1 \rangle e_1 + \dots + \langle v, e_n \rangle e_n\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\|v\|^2 = \lvert \langle v, e_1 \rangle \rvert ^2 + \dots + \lvert \langle v, e_n \rangle \rvert ^2{}\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(\langle u, v \rangle = \langle u, e_1 \rangle \overline{ \langle v, e_1 \rangle } + \cdots + \langle u, e_n \rangle \overline{ \langle v, e_n \rangle }\text{.}\)
