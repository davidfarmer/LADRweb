Exercise 7.

Suppose that \(T \in \Lc(V)\) is self-adjoint or that \(\F{}= \C{}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\) is normal. Let \(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_n\) be the eigenvalues of \(T\text{,}\) each included in this list as many times as the dimension of the corresponding eigenspace. Show that the singular values of \(T\) are \(\lvert \lambda_1 \rvert , \ldots, \lvert \lambda_n \rvert\text{,}\) after these numbers have been sorted into decreasing order.
