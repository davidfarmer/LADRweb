Exercise 5.

Suppose \(m\) and \(n\) are positive integers. For \(v \in \F m\) and \(w \in \FF{n}\text{,}\) identify \(v \tensor w\) with an \(m\)-by-\(n\) matrix as in Example 9.76. With that identification, show that the set
\begin{equation*} \{ v \tensor w : v \in \F m \text{ and } w \in \F n \} \end{equation*}
is the set of \(m\)-by-\(n\) matrices (with entries in \(\F{}\)) that have rank at most one.
