Exercise 12.

An operator \(B \in \Lc(V)\) is called skew if
\begin{equation*} B^* = -B. \end{equation*}
🔗
Suppose that \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Prove that \(T\) is normal if and only if there exist commuting operators \(A\) and \(B\) such that \(A\) is self-adjoint, \(B\) is a skew operator, and \(T = A + B\text{.}\)
