Theorem 2.19. linear dependence lemma.

Suppose \(v_1, \dots, v_m\) is a linearly dependent list in \(V{}\text{.}\) Then there exists \(k \in \{1, 2, \dots, m\}\) such that
\begin{equation*} v_k \in \Span(v_1, \dots, v_{k-1}). \end{equation*}
🔗
Furthermore, if \(k\) satisfies the condition above and the \(k^\nth\) term is removed from \(v_1, \dots, v_m\text{,}\) then the span of the remaining list equals \(\Span(v_1, \dots, v_m)\text{.}\)
