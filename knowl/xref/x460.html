Theorem 4.6. each zero of a polynomial corresponds to a degree-one factor.

Suppose \(m\) is a positive integer and \(p \in \P(\F{})\) is a polynomial of degree \(m\text{.}\) Suppose \(\lambda \in \F{}{}\text{.}\) Then \(p(\lambda) = 0\) if and only if there exists a polynomial \(q \in \P(\F{})\) of degree \(m-1\) such that
\begin{equation*} p(z) = (z-\lambda)q(z) \end{equation*}
for every \(z \in \F{}{}\text{.}\)
