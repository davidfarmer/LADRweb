Exercise 24.

Suppose \(U\) is a finite-dimensional vector space and \(T \in \Lc(U)\text{.}\)
  1. Suppose \(\F{} = \R{}\text{.}\) Prove that \(T\) is diagonalizable if and only if there is a basis of \(U\) such that the matrix of \(T\) with respect to this basis equals its transpose.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Suppose \(\F{} = \C{}\text{.}\) Prove that \(T\) is diagonalizable if and only if there is a basis of \(U\) such that the matrix of \(T\) with respect to this basis commutes with its conjugate transpose.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Note: This exercise adds another equivalence to the list of conditions equivalent to diagonalizability in Theorem 5.49.
