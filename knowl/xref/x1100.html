Exercise 31.

Fix a positive integer \(n\text{.}\) In the inner product space of continuous real-valued functions on \([-\pi,\pi]\) with inner product \(\langle f, g \rangle = \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} fg\text{,}\) let
\begin{equation*} V = \Span( 1, \cos x, \cos 2x, \dots, \cos nx, \sin x, \sin 2x, \dots, \sin nx). \end{equation*}
  1. Define \(D \in \Lc(V)\) by \(Df = f'\!\text{.}\) Show that \(D^* = -D\text{.}\) Conclude that \(D\) is normal but not self-adjoint.
