Theorem 7.32. characterizations of positive operators.

Let \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Then the following are equivalent.
  1. \(T\) is a positive operator.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(T\) is self-adjoint and all eigenvalues of \(T\) are nonnegative.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. With respect to some orthonormal basis of \(V{}\text{,}\) the matrix of \(T\) is a diagonal matrix with only nonnegative numbers on the diagonal.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(T\) has a positive square root.
    🔗
    🔗
  5. \(T\) has a self-adjoint square root.
    🔗
    🔗
  6. \(T = R^*R\) for some \(R \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
