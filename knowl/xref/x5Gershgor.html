Exercise 22.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(A\) is an \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrix that is the matrix of \(T\) with respect to some basis of \(V\text{.}\) Prove that if
\begin{equation*} \lvert A_{j,j} \rvert > \sum_{\scriptstyle k=1 \atop \scriptstyle k\ne j}^n \lvert A_{j,k} \rvert \end{equation*}
for each \(j \in \{ 1, \ldots, n \}\text{,}\) then \(T\) is invertible. Note: This exercise states that if the diagonal entries of the matrix of \(T\) are large compared to the nondiagonal entries, then \(T\) is invertible.
