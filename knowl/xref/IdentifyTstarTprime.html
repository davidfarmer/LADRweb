Exercise 32.

Suppose \(\fun T V W\) is a linear map. Show that under the standard identification of \(V\) with \(V'\) (see 6.61) and the corresponding identification of \(W\) with \(W'\!\text{,}\) the adjoint map \(\fun {T^*} W V\) corresponds to the dual map \(\fun {T'} {W'} {V'}\text{.}\) More precisely, show that
\begin{equation*} T' \! (\phi_w) = \phi_{T^* w} \end{equation*}
for all \(w \in W{}\text{,}\) where \(\phi_w\) and \(\phi_{T^* w}\) are defined as in Theorem 6.61.
