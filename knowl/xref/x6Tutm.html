Exercise 13.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Suppose there exists a subspace \(U\) of \(V\) that is invariant under \(T\) such that \(T|_U\) has an upper-triangular matrix with respect to some basis of \(U\) and also \(T{}/U\) has an upper-triangular matrix with respect to some basis of \(V{}/U\text{.}\) Prove that \(T\) has an upper-triangular matrix with respect to some basis of \(V{}\text{.}\)
