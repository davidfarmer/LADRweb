Theorem 3.70. \(\M(T)_{\cdot, k} = \M(Tv_k)\).

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\) and \(v_1, \dots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V\) and \(w_1, \dots, w_m\) is a basis of \(W{}\text{.}\) Let \(1 \le k \le n\text{.}\) Then the \(k^\nth\) column of \(\M(T)\text{,}\) which is denoted by \(\M(T)_{\cdot, k}\text{,}\) equals \(\M(Tv_k)\text{.}\)
