Theorem 7.110. invertible operator takes parallelepipeds to parallelepipeds.

Suppose \(u \in V\text{,}\) \(v_1, \ldots, v_n\) is a basis of \(V{}\text{,}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\) is invertible. Then
\begin{equation*} T ( u + P(v_1, \ldots, v_n) ) = Tu + P(Tv_1, \ldots, Tv_n). \end{equation*}
