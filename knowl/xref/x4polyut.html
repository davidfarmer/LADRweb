Theorem 5.36. equation satisfied by operator with upper-triangular matrix.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V)\) and \(V\) has a basis with respect to which \(T\) has an upper-triangular matrix with diagonal entries \(\lambda_1, \ldots, \lambda_n\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} (T-\lambda_1 I) \cdots (T - \lambda_n I) = 0. \end{equation*}
