Theorem 7.111. volume changes by a factor of the product of the singular values.

Suppose \(\F{} = \R{}\text{,}\) \(T \in \Lc(V)\) is invertible, and \(\Omega \subset V{}\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} \vol T(\Omega) = (\text{ product of singular values of } T)(\vol \Omega). \end{equation*}
