Theorem 8.37. block diagonal matrix with upper-triangular blocks.

Suppose \(\F{} = \C{}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Let \(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m\) be the distinct eigenvalues of \(T\text{,}\) with multiplicities \(d_1, \dots, d_m\text{.}\) Then there is a basis of \(V\) with respect to which \(T\) has a block diagonal matrix of the form
\begin{equation*} \begin{pmatrix}A_1 \amp \amp 0 \\ \amp \ddots \amp \\ 0 \amp \amp A_m \end{pmatrix} , \end{equation*}
where each \(A_k\) is a \(d_k\)-by-\(d_k\) upper-triangular matrix of the form
\begin{equation*} A_k = \begin{pmatrix}\lambda_k \amp \amp * \\ \amp \ddots \amp \\ 0 \amp \amp \lambda_k \end{pmatrix} . \end{equation*}
