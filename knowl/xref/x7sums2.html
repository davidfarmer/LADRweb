Exercise 11.

Suppose that \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\) and \(v_1, \ldots, v_n\) is an orthonormal basis of \(V{}\text{.}\) Let \(s_1, \ldots, s_n\) denote the singular values of \(T{}\text{.}\)
  1. Prove that \(\lVert Tv_1 \rVert ^2 + \cdots + \lVert Tv_n \rVert ^2 = {s_1}^{2} + \cdots + {s_n}^{2}{}\text{.}\)

    \begin{equation*} \langle Tv_1, v_1 \rangle + \cdots + \langle Tv_n, v_n \rangle = s_1 + \cdots + s_n. \end{equation*}
