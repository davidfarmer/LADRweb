Exercise 13.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional. Prove that every linear map on a subspace of \(V\) can be extended to a linear map on \(V{}\text{.}\) In other words, show that if \(U\) is a subspace of \(V\) and \(S \in \Lc(U, W)\text{,}\) then there exists \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\) such that \(Tu = Su\) for all \(u \in U\text{.}\) Note: The result in this exercise is used in the proof of Theorem 3.126.
