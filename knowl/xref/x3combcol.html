Theorem 3.50. linear combination of columns.

Suppose \(A\) is an \(m\)-by-\(n\) matrix and \(b = \begin{pmatrix}b_1\\ \vdots \\ b_n \end{pmatrix}\) is an \(n\)-by-\(1\) matrix. Then
\begin{equation*} Ab = b_1 A_{\cdot, 1} + \dots + b_n A_{\cdot, n}. \end{equation*}
🔗
In other words, \(Ab\) is a linear combination of the columns of \(A\text{,}\) with the scalars that multiply the columns coming from \(b\text{.}\)
