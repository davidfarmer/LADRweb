Theorem 1.49. condition for a direct sum.

Suppose \(V{}_1, \dots, V{}_m\) are subspaces of \(V{}\text{.}\) Then \(V{}_1 + \dots + V{}_m\) is a direct sum if and only if the only way to write \(0\) as a sum \(v_1 + \dots + v_m\text{,}\) where each \(v_k \in V{}_k\text{,}\) is by taking each \(v_k\) equal to \(0\text{.}\)
