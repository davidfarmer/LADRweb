Theorem 6.45. properties of orthogonal complement.

  1. If \(U\) is a subset of \(V{}\text{,}\) then \(U^\perp\) is a subspace of \(V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\{0\}^\perp = V{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(V^\perp = \{0\}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. If \(U\) is a subset of \(V{}\text{,}\) then \(U \cap U^\perp \subset \{0\}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. If \(G\) and \(H\) are subsets of \(V\) and \(G \subset H\text{,}\) then \(H^\perp \subset G^\perp{}\text{.}\)
