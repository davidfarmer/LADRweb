Theorem 9.35. permutations and alternating multilinear forms.

Suppose \(m\) is a positive integer and \(\alpha \in V_{\text{ alt } }^{(m)}{}\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} \alpha (v_{j_1}, \ldots, v_{j_m}) = ( \sign(j_1, \ldots, j_m) ) \alpha (v_1, \ldots, v_m) \end{equation*}
for every list \(v_1, \ldots, v_m\) of vectors in \(V\) and all \((j_1, \ldots, j_m) \in \perm m\text{.}\)
