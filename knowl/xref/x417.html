Theorem 6.64. minimizing distance to a subspace.

Suppose \(U\) is a finite-dimensional subspace of \(V{}\text{,}\) \(v \in V{}\text{,}\) and \(u \in U\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} \| v - P_{U} v \| \le \| v - u \|. \end{equation*}
🔗
Furthermore, the inequality above is an equality if and only if \(u = P_{U} v\text{.}\)
