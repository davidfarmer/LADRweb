Exercise 8.

Suppose \(A\) is an \(m\)-by-\(n\) matrix and \(B\) is an \(n\)-by-\(p\) matrix. Prove that
\begin{equation*} (AB)_{j,\cdot} = A_{j,\cdot} B \end{equation*}
for each \(1 \le j \le m\text{.}\) In other words, show that row \(j\) of \(AB\) equals (row \(j\) of \(A\)) times \(B\text{.}\) Note: This exercise gives the row version of Theorem 3.45.
