Theorem 5.31. minimal polynomial of a restriction operator.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional, \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{,}\) and \(U\) is a subspace of \(V\) that is invariant under \(T{}\text{.}\) Then the minimal polynomial of \(T\) is a polynomial multiple of the minimal polynomial of \(T|_U\text{.}\)
