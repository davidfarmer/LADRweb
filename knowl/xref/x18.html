Theorem 3.62. injectivity is equivalent to surjectivity (if \(\dim V = \dim W \lt \infty\)).

Suppose that \(V\) and \(W\) are finite-dimensional vector spaces, \(\dim V = \dim W{}\text{,}\) and \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\text{.}\) Then
\begin{equation*} T is invertible \iff T is injective \iff T is surjective. \end{equation*}
