Theorem 5.55. conditions equivalent to diagonalizability.

Suppose \(V\) is finite-dimensional and \(T \in \Lc(V)\text{.}\) Let \(\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m\) denote the distinct eigenvalues of \(T{}\text{.}\) Then the following are equivalent.
  1. \(T\) is diagonalizable.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(V\) has a basis consisting of eigenvectors of \(T{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \(V = E(\lambda_1, T) \oplus \dots \oplus E(\lambda_m, T)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \(\dim V = \dim E(\lambda_1, T) + \dots + \dim E(\lambda_m, T)\text{.}\)
