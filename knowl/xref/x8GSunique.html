Exercise 10.

Suppose \(v_1, \dots, v_m\) is a linearly independent list in \(V{}\text{.}\) Explain why the orthonormal list produced by the formulas of the Gram–Schmidt procedure Theorem 6.32 is the only orthonormal list \(e_1, \ldots, e_m\) in \(V\) such that \(\langle v_k, e_k \rangle > 0\) and \(\Span(v_1, \ldots, v_k) = \Span(e_1, \ldots, e_k)\) for each \(k = 1, \ldots, m\text{.}\) Note: The result in this exercise is used in the proof of Theorem 7.58.
