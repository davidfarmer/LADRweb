Exercise 13.

Suppose \(\F{} = \R{}\) and \(V\) is finite-dimensional. For each \(v \in V{}\text{,}\) let \(\phi_v\) denote the linear functional on \(V\) defined by
\begin{equation*} \phi_v(u) = \langle u, v \rangle \end{equation*}
for all \(u \in V{}\text{.}\)
  1. Show that \(v \mapsto \phi_v\) is an injective linear map from \(V\) to \(V'\!\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Use (a) and a dimension-counting argument to show that \(v \mapsto \phi_v\) is an isomorphism from \(V\) onto \(V'\!\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
Note: The purpose of this exercise is to give an alternative proof of the Riesz representation theorem ( Theorem 6.45 and Theorem 6.61) when \(\F{} = \R{}\text{.}\) Thus you should not use the Riesz representation theorem as a tool in your solution.
