Suppose \(n \ge 3\) and \(\fun \alpha {\F n \times \F n \times \F n} {\F{}}\) is defined by
\begin{align*} \alpha ( (\amp x_1, \ldots, x_n), (y_1, \ldots, y_n), (z_1, \ldots, z_n) )\\ \amp = x_1 y_2 z_3 - x_2 y_1 z_3 - x_3 y_2 z_1 -x_1 y_3 z_2 + x_3 y_1 z_2 + x_2 y_3 z_1\text{.} \end{align*}
🔗
Show that \(\alpha\) is an alternating \(3\)-linear form on \(\FF n\text{.}\)
