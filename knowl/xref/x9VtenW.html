Exercise 4.

Suppose \(\dim V > 1\) and \(\dim W > 1\text{.}\) Prove that
\begin{equation*} \{ v \tensor w : (v, w) \in V{} \times W \} \end{equation*}
is not a subspace of \(V{} \tensor W{}\text{.}\) Note: This exercise implies that if \(\dim V > 1\) and \(\dim W > 1\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \{ v \tensor w : (v, w) \in V{} \times W \} \ne V{} \tensor W{}. \end{equation*}
