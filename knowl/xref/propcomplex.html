Theorem 4.4. properties of complex numbers.

Suppose \(w, z \in \C{}\text{.}\) Then the following equalities and inequalities hold.
  1. sum of \(z\) and \(\bar{z}\).
    \(z + \bar{z} = 2 \re z\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. difference of \(z\) and \(\bar{z}\).
    \(z - \bar{z} = 2( \im z)i\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. product of \(z\) and \(\bar{z}\).
    \(z \bar{z} = |z|^2{}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. additivity and multiplicativity of complex conjugate.
    \(\overline{w + z} = \bar{w} + \bar{z}\) and \(\overline{wz} = \bar{w} \; \bar{z}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  5. double complex conjugate.
    \(\overline{\bar{z}} = z\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. real and imaginary parts are bounded by \(|z|\).
    \(| \re z | \le |z|\) and \(| \im z | \le |z|\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  7. absolute value of the complex conjugate.
    \(\lvert \bar{z} \rvert = |z|\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  8. multiplicativity of absolute value.
    \(|wz| = |w| |z|\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  9. triangle inequality.
    TriangleInequality.png \(|w + z| \le |w| + |z|\text{.}\)
