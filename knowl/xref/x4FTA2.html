Theorem 4.13. fundamental theorem of algebra, second version.

If \(p \in \P(\C{})\) is a nonconstant polynomial, then \(p\) has a unique factorization (except for the order of the factors) of the form
\begin{equation*} p(z) = c(z - \lambda_1) \dotsm (z - \lambda_m), \end{equation*}
where \(c, \lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m \in \C{}\text{.}\)
