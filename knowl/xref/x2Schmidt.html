Exercise 2.

Suppose \(T \in \Lc(V{}, W)\) and \(s > 0\text{.}\) Prove that \(s\) is a singular value of \(T\) if and only if there exist nonzero vectors \(v \in V\) and \(w \in W\) such that
\begin{equation*} Tv = sw \andd T^* w = sv. \end{equation*}
🔗
Note: The vectors \(v, w\) satisfying both equations above are called a Schmidt pair. Erhard Schmidt introduced the concept of singular values in 1907.
